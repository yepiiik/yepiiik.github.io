How you ended up in Tech?

## Why Have You Started Coding? My first encounter with coding was in middle school when I decided to create a website for workout plans. I was 11 years old and did it for fun. It started with HTML, CSS and the more I tried to build the more I wanted to learn. Later on, I got into JavaScript and then Python, PHP, Java, Kotlin, C++... What I have noticed is that the friction of the challenges that I've been making up for myself taught me more than any courses or tutorials. By the age of 17 when I was deciding on what to study in university I've already was self-taught and knew that university won't teach me how to code, therefore I was perceiving it as a place where I can meet like-minded people. Now I am finishing my bachelor's degree in Information and Communication Technologies and I proved that university has much more sense when it comes to networking with soulmates.
